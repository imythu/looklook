Looklook client 1.4.0: faster and safer remote access.

- When you open your computer remotely, your browser can now connect directly to a nearby relay server instead of going through the Looklook platform, so terminals and local pages open faster.
- Remote access verification has been upgraded: every request carries a time-limited credential issued by the platform, and it stops working as soon as you sign out or disable a local page.
- A remotely opened page that has been left open for a long time now refreshes itself once when its sign-in expires, instead of showing errors.
- Remote access in this version is not compatible with older clients. Please upgrade soon; older clients cannot be opened remotely until they are upgraded.

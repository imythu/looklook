Looklook desktop 1.18.0: the remote console and terminals now use an end-to-end encrypted connection between your browser and your computer, so relay servers can no longer see your terminal, and typing is faster.

- When you open the console remotely, the browser and your computer connect over end-to-end encrypted WebRTC: directly when possible (same network, or NAT traversal succeeds), otherwise through your relay, which only sees encrypted data
- Typing and output in the terminal are noticeably faster over a direct connection
- Signing out or ending a session on the web now closes remote pages that are already open right away
- This is a required update: older versions of the desktop app can no longer be opened remotely

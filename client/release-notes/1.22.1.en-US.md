looklook desktop 1.22.1: terminals opened remotely on a phone now reconnect on their own after a drop.

- After switching away or locking the phone, terminals reconnect automatically when you come back; no need to press Enter or refresh.
- The reconnect notice is now a small pill at the top that stays out of the way for quick reconnects, with a "Retry now" button when it takes longer.
- The terminal page no longer turns into "Something went wrong, please try again later" while reconnecting.

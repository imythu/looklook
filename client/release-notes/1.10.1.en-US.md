Looklook client 1.10.1: with several terminal windows open on your computer, the one you click comes to the front, and floating windows can switch to your local network too.

- Floating terminal windows now stack like desktop windows: click one (or click into its terminal) to bring it to the front, and clicking Open on a terminal that's already open brings its window forward. The front window has a brighter border so you can tell which one you're using.
- When you open Looklook remotely in a desktop browser on the same network as your computer, the floating terminal's title bar now has the local network button too. It opens that terminal over your local network in a new tab, so typing feels snappier.
- The launch title on terminal cards no longer wraps onto two lines.

Looklook desktop 1.31.1: fixes Looklook not restarting on its own after an update on Mac.

- Mac: after an update is installed (by clicking Update in Settings, or automatically), Looklook now reopens on its own; you no longer have to start it again from the Dock or Launchpad. Updating from 1.31.0 or earlier to this version still needs one manual reopen.

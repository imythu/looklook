LookLook desktop 1.45.1: fixes for chats that stopped responding to sending, stopping or starting a new conversation.

- Fixed: with many sessions open, sending a message, stopping or starting a new session in the chat did nothing until you closed a few tabs. All chats now share one connection, so any number of open sessions works.
- Fixed: when Codex couldn't reopen a conversation (for example the Codex pre-release error “list_turns is not supported yet”), the whole chat was stuck on “Codex couldn't start” and retrying didn't help. It now says the conversation can't be reopened, shows Codex's reason, and lets you start a new conversation, open an earlier one or try again.
- Fixed: in Codex terminals with full access, switching conversations with /resume failed with “Permission overrides are not supported when resuming a remote task.” Switching works now, and full access still applies.

Looklook desktop 1.31.0: when you open your computers remotely, they no longer keep showing up as "offline", connections are faster and steadier, and you're told why when one can't be reached. Codex's terminal and chat now share one session.

- Your computer keeps a standing connection to Looklook, and remote connection requests now reach it directly over that link instead of going through the relay server. Previously, a small network hiccup could make an online computer show as offline and grey out its sessions; now it only shows offline when it has really disconnected.
- On the remote page each computer shows Connecting, Online, Reconnecting or Can't connect. When it can't connect, the reason is shown with a one-click Retry. Background browser tabs no longer keep reconnecting.
- The connection type in the computer menu no longer sometimes shows as "Relay ?", and the related errors no longer fill the log.
- Codex: the chat view and the Terminal tab are now the same session. Messages sent and approvals answered in the terminal show up in the chat live, and the other way round; the Codex terminal stays connected when Looklook restarts.
- Claude Code: the chat view no longer loses earlier messages after the context is auto-compacted, shows "Compacting context…" while it compacts, and clears the input box right away when you send slash commands such as /clear or /new instead of saying the message wasn't delivered.

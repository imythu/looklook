LookLook desktop 1.46.0: notifications in WeCom, DingTalk and WeChat, and more Hermes web UIs recognized.

- New: “More notification channels” under Settings → Notifications. When an AI agent needs you, finishes, fails or gets stuck, you can be notified in a WeCom or DingTalk group, on personal WeChat via ServerChan or PushPlus, or on your phone via Bark or ntfy, and you can add several at once. These channels only notify: tap the link in the message to open LookLook and respond. Quiet hours, batching, stuck reminders and muted projects apply too. Webhook URLs and keys stay on your computers, which send the messages directly.
- New: “Ports listening on this computer” in Local pages now recognizes Hermes WebUI, Hermes Workspace and Hermes Studio, so you can turn them into a page only you can open. The Hermes gateway API is flagged with a reminder to keep it on localhost.

LookLook desktop 1.55.0: set up Feishu with one scan, a smoother AI chat in Feishu, and AI assistants that can read and send Feishu messages as you.

- New: Feishu setup by scan. In Settings → Notifications, click "Scan with Feishu to create the bot", scan with Feishu (or tap "Open link" on a phone) and confirm: the app, permissions, events, callbacks and binding are all set up at once, no more filling in the developer console. Already connected? Use "Scan to add missing permissions". The old manual setup is still there under "Set up manually".
- Improved: Just message the bot to keep talking to the current session; no need to reply inside a topic. Send /new or tap "New session" on a card to start a new one, in the same project as last time without asking every time.
- New: Your message gets a "typing" reaction right away, removed when the turn is done.
- Improved: Redesigned answer cards: no big header, so they read like a message; the tools used and the thinking sit in collapsible panels; longer answers fit; the footer shows the time taken and "Claude · project · computer".
- New: Send "stop" to stop the current turn. Messages sent within a second are combined into one; images or files sent alone are held until you say what to do with them.
- New: Forwarded chat history is expanded into text for the AI, and replying to a message brings its text along.
- New: Project groups. Send /group and the bot creates a topic group where every new topic is a new session, no @ needed.
- New: If a permission is missing, you get a card in Feishu to grant it right there.
- New: Feishu tools for AI assistants (Claude Code, Codex…): read your group chats, direct chats and topics as you, download their images and files, list group members, find people, search and read docs, and send messages as you. Authorize once in Feishu the first time; every message sent as you is confirmed with a card in Feishu first and only goes out if you tap Send. Turn it off or revoke it on the AI tools & access page.

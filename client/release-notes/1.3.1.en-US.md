Looklook client 1.3.1: brings back the terminal key bar on phones and tablets.

- Fixed: since 1.3.0, terminals opened on a phone or tablet had no key bar (Esc, Tab, Ctrl, arrow keys and the shortcuts panel). On touch devices, "Open here" and "Open in new tab" (or "Open in new window" on a terminal card) now open the full-page terminal, which has the key bar. Nothing changes on computers.

Looklook desktop 1.29.0: remote terminals in the browser are steadier and survive network changes, session tabs no longer disappear after reconnecting, and new sessions use the permissions you prefer.

- When the console is opened remotely in a browser, terminals now carry on over the new connection after the phone goes to the background and back, or switches between Wi-Fi and mobile data: no "Reconnecting" message, no full-screen redraw, and output from the gap is filled in. This needs the Looklook website update as well (already live).
- If the network silently stops working (Wi-Fi connected but no internet), the browser switches to a new connection in about 20 seconds instead of half a minute or more.
- Session tabs at the top no longer disappear after reconnecting or switching "This computer only". Tabs for sessions that were deleted close automatically when the page is reopened.
- The + on a project now starts Claude Code, Codex and OpenCode sessions with or without full access to match the most recent session in that project. New projects follow Settings → Terminal → Permissions for new AI sessions (default: ask when needed).

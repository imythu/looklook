looklook client 1.25.0: one-tap newline on phones, and a "this computer only" mode in the App.

- On phones, terminals running Claude Code, Codex or OpenCode now have a Newline key on the key bar, so you can add a line to the prompt with one tap.
- On phones, Enter in the new-session box now adds a line; tap Start to send.
- New "This computer only" switch in the App (sidebar computer menu or Settings → Network & access): it hides your other computers and connects straight to this one, so their reconnects no longer get in the way.

Looklook client 1.0.1 beta: safer sign-in and access, and a smoother desktop experience.

- Sign-in is now confirmed in your browser: the client shows a code, you click "Allow" on the web page, and you no longer type your password into the client.
- Access control: choose whether devices on the same Wi-Fi, or specific IPs, may open the console, and optionally protect it with an access code. Opening it on the computer itself never needs the code. On machines without a desktop, such as a NAS, use the looklook access command.
- The "Open on your phone or another computer" card now has a QR code, and lets you switch between "Anywhere" (your personal address) and "Same Wi-Fi" (the local network address).
- A folder picker when creating a terminal; an "Open with all permissions" option for Codex and Claude Code; terminals can be embedded in the page, and run history is clearer.
- Windows and macOS get a tray icon: Open, Start, Stop, Quit and Launch at startup. Windows no longer leaves a black console window open.
- Several display problems on phones are fixed (text squeezed into one column, overflowing buttons); a running terminal can no longer be deleted by mistake.
- Smaller installers, plus stronger anti-debugging and integrity protection.
- Installers are still not code-signed: Windows and macOS need a manual allow on first run, see the Download page.

LookLook desktop 1.45.2: collapse the session sidebar, and quick switch no longer takes over a conversation that's still being written elsewhere.

- Improved: a “Collapse sidebar” button sits next to the session list title; click “Sessions” in the icon bar to bring it back. Ctrl+Shift+S only works on session pages, so pressing it on a tool page no longer quietly remembers the sidebar as collapsed.
- Improved: while searching or filtering by status, projects can be folded too (only for that search or filter; your usual folding stays as it was).
- Fixed: in Ctrl+K quick switch and “Recent conversations” on the new session page, clicking a conversation that was still being written in the last two minutes continued it right away, so two places could write to it at once. Like the sidebar, it now opens the project page first, and shows the reason if continuing fails.

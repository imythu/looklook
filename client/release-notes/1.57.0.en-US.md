Looklook desktop 1.57.0: no account needed. Everything on this computer and on your Wi‑Fi is free and works without signing in. To keep going from your phone while you're out, turn on remote access; sign up during the beta and it's free for a year.

- New: Use Looklook right after installing, without signing in. Terminals, AI chat, files, Feishu and other notifications, and MCP all work. Turn on LAN access under "Settings → Access control" and phones on the same Wi‑Fi can open it too.
- New: "Go remote" at the top of the console and "Turn on remote access" in the menu sign you in, so you can open this computer from anywhere with your personal address. Sign up during the beta and remote access is free for a year. After that it's tentatively $7.77/month (¥9.99 in China); the price may change with server costs when paid plans open, and payment isn't open yet.
- Improved: When your membership expires or this computer has been offline too long, only remote access is paused. Terminals and AI sessions keep running, and Looklook keeps working on this computer and your LAN. Remote access comes back as soon as you extend your membership. Previously, expiry stopped all terminals; it no longer does.
- New: The console reminds you before your membership expires, with a "Remind me later" option.
- Improved: When you're not signed in, Feishu cards and push notifications say that "Open" only works on this computer and include a link to turn on remote access. Buttons on Feishu cards such as Allow and Deny still work.
- Improved: `looklook status` now says whether you're using Looklook only on this computer and LAN, or whether remote access is on or paused.

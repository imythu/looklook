Looklook desktop 1.21.0: the app and the remote console in your browser are now the same, and account tasks no longer need the website.

- New Computers, Membership & invites, and Account & security pages in the user menu: redeem renewal codes, create invite links, change your name or password, sign out unfamiliar browsers, and sign out a computer, all from here
- The desktop app can switch to other computers on the same account and use their sessions, files and settings, over an end-to-end encrypted connection
- One connection badge in the top bar: on this computer it shows the remote channel and lets you reconnect or change routes; remotely it shows latency and which computer you're connected to
- The user menu has one-click links to the Looklook home page, the docs and the GitHub repository

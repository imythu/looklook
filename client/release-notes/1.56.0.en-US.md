LookLook desktop 1.56.0: a new terminal engine, so full-screen redraws in Claude Code, Codex and others no longer flicker, and long-press text selection on phones is harder to trigger by accident.

- Improved: The terminal now runs on xterm.js 6. AI coding assistants' full-screen redraws are shown only once complete, so no more flicker or tearing, even on slow connections. Drawing uses the GPU for smoother output (falls back automatically when the GPU is unavailable).
- New: When a program reports progress (command-line tools that support terminal progress bars), a thin bar appears at the top of the terminal: red on error, yellow when paused.
- Improved: Hyperlinks printed by programs (OSC 8) now work through the persistent session too.
- Improved: Long-press to select text on phones no longer vibrates. Hold for half a second and the row under your finger lights up; "Select text" opens when you lift your finger. If you start swiping instead, it's treated as scrolling.
- Improved: "Select text" opened by a long press now scrolls to the row you held and briefly highlights it, even in older output you scrolled back to, so you don't have to search up from the bottom.

LookLook desktop 1.53.1: domains in Direct connection settings now resolve even with a proxy app running.

- Fixed: When the public IPv4 / IPv6 in Direct connection settings was a domain (DDNS) and the computer ran a proxy app such as Clash / mihomo in fake-ip mode, the domain stayed "not resolved yet" and was never used. When the system DNS gives no real address, LookLook now asks public DNS (AliDNS, Cloudflare).

Looklook client 1.8.0: terminals connect directly when you open your remote address on the same computer.

- When you open the console from your remote address in a browser on the computer that runs the terminals, terminals now connect to it directly instead of going through the remote route, so typing has almost no delay. A green "Direct" label appears next to the terminal title.
- The first time, recent Chrome and Edge ask whether the page may "access apps and services on this device". Choose Allow. If you decline, terminals keep using the remote route and you won't be asked again. Only desktop browsers try this, so phones never see the prompt.
- When a terminal goes through the remote route, its title shows the round-trip delay in milliseconds, in amber when it's slow. Hover over it to see where the delay comes from and how to improve it.
- Reduced occasional output stutter in terminals on macOS and Windows.

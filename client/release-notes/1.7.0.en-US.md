Looklook client 1.7.0: automatic route picks the fastest one, scroll the terminal on your phone, rename your computers.

- "Automatic" route selection now goes by latency: this computer tests the routes regularly and moves to a clearly faster one. It only switches when no terminal is open, so it never interrupts a session. A route you picked yourself is never changed automatically.
- Route settings moved to the Settings page (the Account page links there). If your route gets noticeably slow while another is much faster, a short notice at the top of the page tells you where to switch. Dismiss it with × and it stays away for 24 hours. The notice never appears inside a terminal.
- On phones you can now swipe the terminal up and down to read earlier output, for AI assistants like Codex and Claude Code as well as the regular command line, with momentum when you let go.
- Rename your computers: when you open remotely with several computers, tap ✏️ next to a computer's name in the session list, or rename it under "My computers" on the Looklook website. Names stay in sync both ways.

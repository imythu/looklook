LookLook desktop 1.54.0: a getting-started guide, one-time tips, a chat walkthrough, and help links on every page.

- New: A getting-started checklist. Right after installing you see 5 steps: open a project, have AI do something, open it on your phone, get a notification, check Tasks. Steps tick themselves off as you do them; click "Skip, I know my way around" to put it away, and reopen it any time from "Getting started" in the user menu.
- New: One-time tips. The first time you reach a feature (the "Waiting for you" group, background sessions, switching between terminal and chat, uploading files and more), a small bubble explains it; click "Got it" and it never comes back. Only one shows at a time. Which tips you've seen syncs across all your computers, and you can show them all again in Settings → General → Tips.
- New: A short walkthrough the first time you open a chat: sending, queuing and interrupting, permission modes, slash commands and the side panel.
- Improved: The browser no longer asks for notification permission the moment you send a message; it asks the first time something needs your approval, or when a turn runs longer than a minute.
- New: Rotating tips on the new-session page, and "What else can it do?" at the bottom of the checklist: 12 handy but easy-to-miss features, one click away.
- New: Help links next to the titles of Files, SSH hosts, Local pages, AI tools, Tasks and Chat history open the matching docs. The docs site also gained pages for chat, tasks, files, SSH hosts, AI tools & models and keyboard shortcuts.
- Improved: An empty Tasks page now explains what it's for, with a button to start a session.
- Improved: The proxy-app rules on the route card now let LookLook's UDP go direct by process (so peer-to-peer traffic isn't captured by the proxy either), and explain when to add them and when not to.
- Fixed: Opening a Claude session showed a blank white page when the chat side panel was left on the Usage tab (most often hit on phones).

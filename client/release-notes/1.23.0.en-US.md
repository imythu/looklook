Looklook desktop 1.23.0: continue conversations you had with Claude Code, Codex or OpenCode elsewhere.

- The new-session start page lists the project's earlier conversations. Open one to see its last few messages, then click Continue to pick it up in Looklook.
- The clock button next to New session opens all earlier conversations, grouped by folder and searchable. A folder that isn't a project yet becomes one once you continue a conversation in it.
- The project ⋯ menu and the sidebar search also lead to earlier conversations.
- A conversation that is already open in Looklook opens that session. One that may still be open in another terminal is continued in a copy by default, so the original stays untouched.

LookLook desktop 1.44.0: Feishu notifications. When you're away and the AI needs you, finishes or fails, you hear about it in Feishu and can respond right on the card.

- New: a Notifications section in Settings walks you through creating a Feishu app and binding your account. It's your own Feishu app, and your computer sends messages straight to Feishu, not through LookLook's servers.
- New: when the AI needs approval, asks a question or waits for a choice, a card arrives in your Feishu chat. Allow, deny, tap an option, or type an answer. Risky commands such as deleting folders or force-pushing are flagged red and ask once more before you allow them.
- New: get a card when a turn finishes (after 30 seconds or more) or fails, and reply on the card to keep the AI going.
- New: once something is handled in LookLook or the terminal, the Feishu card changes to “handled”, so no stale cards are left behind.
- New: turn off “done” or “failed” notifications, mute projects, switch to notify-only, or hide commands and replies on cards. Nothing is sent while you have that session open.
- With several computers, a button tapped in Feishu is passed to the computer running that session, and the Feishu setup syncs to your other computers through settings sync.

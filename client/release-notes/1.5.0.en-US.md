Looklook client 1.5.0: up to 7 computers per account, and file transfer plus image paste for AI right in the terminal.

- One account can now be signed in on up to 7 computers at once. When you open Looklook remotely, the sessions of all your computers are shown on one page, grouped by computer; local pages, settings and similar pages have a computer switcher at the top.
- Different computers can each add the same local port as a local page.
- When 7 computers are already signed in, a new one is refused and the signed-in computers are listed; sign one out under "My computers" on the website, then sign in again. Signing in again on the same computer doesn't use a new slot.
- New "Files" button in the terminal header: upload files to the folder the terminal is currently in; on phones you can also pick photos. Large files are sent in pieces and resume automatically after a network drop.
- On a computer you can drag files onto the terminal, or press Ctrl+V to paste a screenshot or copied files.
- In Codex and Claude Code, pasted or uploaded images are handed to the AI as images. They are kept in Looklook's own temporary folder and removed after 7 days, so they never end up in your project.
- Running rz in a terminal opens the upload panel automatically, and sz offers downloads. trz / tsz (trzsz) are included too, for transferring files after you ssh to another machine.
- Auto-update: a new version is installed once no terminal has been open for 10 minutes (can be turned off in Settings); headless machines can use `looklook update`.
- New System page: CPU, memory, disks, network and the busiest processes.
- Codex and Claude Code can add and reuse local pages through a local MCP server (one-click install on the Local pages screen, off by default).
- Faster remote access: your browser connects directly to a relay server instead of going through the Looklook platform.
- Codex now always opens as a terminal; the old chat mode is gone.
- Remote access in this version is not compatible with older clients. Please upgrade; older clients cannot be opened remotely until they are upgraded.

Looklook desktop 1.18.2: if your network blocks UDP and remote access can't work, the app now tells you; the allowlist accepts domains.

- When the remote tunnel is connected but this computer's network blocks UDP, the status now shows "Remote limited" and explains why the remote console can't open and what to do, instead of "Remote on". It clears within a minute once UDP works again
- The console allowlist accepts domain names (such as a home DDNS name) as well as IPs and ranges. Looklook resolves them regularly and lets in visitors whose IP matches; the settings page shows what each domain currently resolves to

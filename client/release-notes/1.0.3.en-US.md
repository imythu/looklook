Looklook client 1.0.3: the first stable release, with a much easier terminal on your phone.

- New key bar at the bottom of the terminal on phones and tablets: Esc, Tab, Ctrl, Alt, Shift, arrow keys, Home/End, Ctrl+C and Paste are always there. "More" adds common shortcuts (each with a short label such as "Interrupt", "Clear" or "History"), symbols that are hard to type on a phone keyboard (| ~ / \ { } and more), paging keys and F1–F12.
- Ctrl / Alt / Shift: tap once for the next key, tap again to lock, a third time to release. They also work with your phone's own keyboard — tap Ctrl, then type r for Ctrl+R.
- Hold arrow, delete and paging keys to repeat. Tapping the key bar doesn't open or close your phone keyboard, and in landscape the bar shrinks to a single row to leave more room for the terminal.
- The key bar appears only on phones and tablets, not on computers. Use the keyboard button at the top of the terminal to hide or show it.
- Includes everything from the 1.0.x betas: remote tunnel status with plain-language failure reasons, terminal connecting and auto-reconnect prompts, a draggable floating terminal window, tray menu items grayed out by service state, a fully translated English interface, and more.

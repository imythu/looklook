Looklook client 1.10.0: look at files right from the terminal, switch to your local network when you're at home, and one stuck computer no longer holds up your session list.

- A new Files tab on the terminal page starts in the terminal's current folder. Preview text, code and images, download files, or insert a file's path into the terminal (handy for your AI assistant).
- When your phone and computer are on the same network, the remote page offers "Use local network": one tap opens a direct local connection in a new tab, so typing feels snappier. You can have it switch automatically, and it offers the remote address again when you leave the network. Turn it off under Settings → Access if you don't want it.
- With several computers signed in, each one's sessions load on their own: if one computer is stuck or not responding, the others still show right away and only the stuck one shows a notice.
- Each route under Settings → Remote route now shows its country and region.
